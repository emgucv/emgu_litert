# Agents and the reusable chat

`Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents` (the chat agent's tools) and `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat`, `.Chat.Sqlite` and `.Maui` (the reusable chat). Moved here from CLAUDE.md, which keeps the short version.

## Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents

- `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents/` (own `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.dll` and nuget package, `EMGU_LITERT_LM_AGENTS_NUGET_ID`, props/README from `cmake/Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.*.in`; netstandard2.0 + net8.0, pure managed; namespace `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents`; its only dependency is `Microsoft.Extensions.AI.Abstractions`, which brings System.Text.Json for netstandard2.0 - a direct reference to an older one failed restore with NU1605) holds the chat agent's reusable tools, moved out of the MAUI demo so any app - with any `IChatClient` - can use them: `AgentTools.CurrentDateTime()`, `Calculate()` (with the public `Calculator.Evaluate`/`Format`), `Memory(IAgentMemoryStore)` (remember/forget; `InMemoryAgentMemoryStore`; `MemoryInstructionsAsync(store, maxLength)` for the agent's instructions - or `MemoriesForInstructions` and `MemoryInstructions` to keep the list given; `MemoriesUsed(given, message, reply)` says which of them a reply drew on, by words: a memory with a distinctive word in the reply that the message didn't have, or two in the reply (an estimate: chance overlaps count, a memory used in other words or another language doesn't; `UnitTestMemoriesUsed`); remember skips a fact whose content words - minus stop words, crude singulars - are all in a saved one, and forget deletes facts containing the text or all its content words, since the model rewords them: "I love hiking in the Alps" didn't match "The user loves hiking in the Alps." as a substring), `ConvertUnits()` (`UnitConverter`: length, mass, volume - US customary and imperial gallons/pints - temperature, speed, area, time, data - kB/KiB - energy, pressure; symbols, names and plurals), `ConvertCurrency(http)` (Frankfurter's ECB reference rates, `api.frankfurter.dev/v1` - the old `api.frankfurter.app` redirects there - ~30 currencies, common names/symbols mapped to ISO codes), `ReadWebPage(http, maxLength)` (`read_web_page` without a key: fetches http(s) pages up to 3 MB and keeps `HtmlText.Extract`'s title and main text - scripts, styles, nav, header, footer, aside, forms dropped; tables too unless the page has little other text, since Wikipedia's infobox used the whole budget; tag patterns skip quoted attributes, since Wikipedia's JSON `data-mw` attributes contain '>'; citation markers removed; `<article>`/`<main>` if present, else paragraphs/headings/list items), `Location(lookup)` (the app supplies the platform lookup), `GenerateImage(generate)` (`generate_image`: an English description of at most ~20 words, which the app's generator - the demo's Z-Image-Turbo - draws and shows to the user; step line "Drawing “...”"), `FindPhotos(search)` (`find_photos`: one `request` in the user's words, which the app's search turns into a `PhotoQuery` with `PhotoRequest.Parse(request, now, knownPlaces, knownCameras)` - description, place, from/to, kind, camera - and shows the photos; the model doesn't see them. One text because Gemma 4 E2B filled separate fields unreliably: "beach" as the place, invented cameras and kinds, asking for dates - 8/8 requests were passed on faithfully as one text. The parser handles today/yesterday/this or last week, weekend, month, year, seasons (northern hemisphere; "last" skips a season still under way), months with or without a year, "since/after/before 2023", years, "N weeks ago", "last N days"; kinds selfie/screenshot/panorama/favo(u)rite/portrait mode; places from the app's list or a capitalised name after in/from/at/to, plus country aliases (USA, UK, Holland...); cameras from the app's list or brand words; travel words dropped with a place; the rest minus filler words is the description), `PlaceLookup` (offline reverse geocoding from a gzip TSV of towns - the demo ships GeoNames' towns of 5,000+ people, CC BY 4.0, built by `Emgu.LiteRT.Example/Maui/tools/make_places.py` into `Resources/Raw/places.tsv.gz`, 1.1 MB, without PPLX/PPLH/PPLQ/PPLW; loads in ~0.1 s, lookups well under 1 ms through a 1° grid; the nearest town wins unless a more populous one is within 2 × its distance + 3 km, since big cities' districts are towns of their own - GeoNames' "Paris 16 Passy" was nearer the Eiffel Tower than central Paris; `Matches` ignores case, accents and punctuation), `Weather(http)` (Open-Meteo; `place`/`latitude`/`longitude` have defaults - without them `AIFunctionFactory` made the coordinates required), `Wikipedia(http)`, `TavilySearch(key, http)`/`TavilyReadPage(key, http)`, `CreateDocument(save, userRequest)` (`create_document`: title, Markdown content, format; `DocumentWriter.cs` writes Word .docx - Office Open XML by hand - PDF - a small A4 writer with the standard Helvetica/Courier fonts and WinAnsi text, falling back to Word for other scripts - or Markdown; the user's words choose the format first (`ChooseFormat`), and Markdown only when asked; the demo's `LiteRtLmChatPage.WrittenDocuments.cs` shows a card with Open/Share and saves the file with the reply as a `document` attachment, name in the media type; `UnitTestDocumentWriter`, TODO item 18), `ToolRouter`/`ToolGroup` (`ToolRouter.cs`: tool groups on demand - an app's groups come when a message's words need them (keywords at word starts - a keyword ending in `$` must end a word, so "a week$" misses "a weekend" - or `ToolGroup.AlsoMatches`) or the app calls `Activate`, and stay until `Reset` (a new conversation: LiteRT-LM re-reads the conversation when the tools change); every other group is a `use_{name}` stub plus one instruction line listing them, and calling a stub adds its tools and instructions to the same request through the options `currentOptions` returns - `FunctionInvokingChatClient.CurrentContext.Options`, which the function invocation re-reads each round - so the model calls them straight away; a group with `rerunAfterStub` (the demo's clock/calendar/maps) instead ends the request (`endRequest`) and `TakeRerun` tells the app to send the message again with the group - the demo sends it again into the same reply with `ChatConversation.RegenerateAsync` - since after the stub's turn Gemma 4 E2B asked for calendar details it had been given, while the camera planner did better continuing; the demo's groups are `ChatToolGroups.cs` + `LiteRtLmChatPage.Tools.cs`; `UnitTestToolRouting`, TODO item 37), `Goals(store)` (`AgentGoals.cs`: `add_goal`, `log_progress`, `get_goals`, `remove_goal` over an app's `IAgentGoalStore` - `InMemoryAgentGoalStore` - with the goal in the user's words: `GoalParser.Parse` reads target/unit/period ("three times a week", "20 pages a day", "4x per week"; none for "Learn Spanish") and `TryParsePastDay` the day progress was made ("yesterday", "last night", "Monday" - the last one); `log_progress` finds the goal by its words, ignoring numbers and period words; weeks start on Monday; `GoalInstructionsAsync` lists each goal with this period's progress for the agent's instructions; `UnitTestAgentGoals`; `log_progress` takes the amount's unit too, since Gemma 4 E2B passed the 5 of "a run, 5 km" for a goal counted in times - another unit counts as one time; the demo stores them in `ChatStore.Goals.cs` (in `Maui.Demo.Lite.Core`, as `ChatStore.cs` is) with "Remember things about me" and lists them in Settings > Your goals - Edit, Undo last, Delete - TODO item 25), `DeviceActions(kinds, perform)` (`DeviceActions.cs`: `set_timer`, `set_alarm`, `add_calendar_event`, `add_reminder`, `open_directions` - the model passes times in the user's words, read by `WhenParser` (`TryParseDuration`, `TryParseDateTime(text, now, ...)`: "half an hour", "tomorrow at 6:45 am", "next Friday at noon", "3rd of March"; an hour without am/pm is the next one to come), since small models get exact dates wrong; unreadable or past times go back to the model; the app's `perform` gets a typed `DeviceActionRequest` (`Title`, `Details`, `ConfirmText`), should ask the user, and returns `DeviceActionDone`/`DeviceActionCancelled`/`DeviceActionFailed`; `DeviceActionInstructions(kinds)` belongs in the agent's instructions - without it Gemma 4 26B-A4B with thinking off said "I've set your alarm" without calling the tool; `UnitTestDeviceActions`), and `Describe(call)` for step lines; `AgentTools.DefaultHttpClient` (20 s, a User-Agent naming the package) unless an `HttpClient` is passed; request bodies via `Utf8JsonWriter`, `GetAsync`/`ReadAsStringAsync()` (netstandard2.0 has no `GetStringAsync(url, ct)`).
- `ChatOptions.Tools` is null by default, so examples use `Tools = new List<AITool> { ... }` (a collection initializer alone throws).
- Tests: `TestCalculator`, `TestUnitConverter`, `TestHtmlText`, `TestAgentMemoryTools` (no model, run by default), `TestAgentWebTools` (network: weather, Wikipedia, currency, the Ada Lovelace article read without its infobox or JSON) and `TestAgentTools` (Gemma 4 E2B calls `calculate` - 838,102.05 - `get_weather`, `convert_units` - 42.16 km - `convert_currency` and `read_web_page`; given a Wikipedia link it searched Wikipedia instead, so the page test uses python.org), the last two `TEST_MODELS`-gated.

## Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat, .Chat.Sqlite and .Maui

- `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat/` and `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Maui/` are the reusable chat being extracted from the MAUI demo's chat page, per `Emgu.LiteRT.Example/Maui/history/CHAT_LIBRARY_PLAN.md` (all 7 steps done; the package READMEs `cmake/Emgu.LiteRT.LM.{Chat,Maui}.Package.README.md.in` describe the API, restyling by key and the `PART_` contract - their C# examples were compiled, and need the `Microsoft.Extensions.AI` package for `ChatClientBuilder`: the demo's chat page now runs on `ChatConversation` and shows its transcript in a `ChatView` whose demo `ChatViewTemplate` has only the transcript - the page keeps its own message box, chips, model card and settings - with the demo's `ChatUserMessageTemplate`/`ChatReplyTemplate`/`ChatAttachmentViewTemplate` in `ChatTemplates.xaml` binding to the page's data in each message's `Tag` (`UserMessageInfo`, `StreamingBubble`); feature cards are the reply's `Decorations` (`AddCard`/`RemoveCard`) or notices; scaffolding; attachments - `IChatAttachmentSource`/`MediaChatAttachmentSource`, `ChatImages.PrepareAsync` (1024 px JPEG, EXIF orientation applied by hand on Android), `ChatAttachmentView` - and read-aloud - `IReplySpeaker`/`TextToSpeechReplySpeaker` over `ReplySpeechText` and the Agents package's `SpeechSegmenter`; `ChatView` - a templated chat over a `Model` and/or `ChatClient`, with `ModelLoadCard` over `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat`'s `ModelLoadState`, messages as `ChatUserMessageTemplate`/`ChatReplyTemplate` DataTemplates, Send/Stop styled by key, the transcript's views made by `ChatView` itself (`RenderTranscript`: about a frame's worth at a time, newest first - a `BindableLayout` built a saved 120-message chat's views at once, holding a Galaxy S10's UI thread for 19 s; now 0.6 s) - and the sample app `Emgu.LiteRT.Example/MauiChat/` (namespace `MauiChat`, since under `Emgu.LiteRT.*` `Environment` resolves to `Emgu.LiteRT.Environment`; Qwen3, restyles the Send button and the user's messages), checked on Mac Catalyst and the Pixel 8 emulator; LiteRT's XNNPACK weight cache aborts the process when the disk is full; `ChatConversation` - messages as `ChatMessageItem`s, streaming over any `IChatClient`, compaction by summary or dropping, the context-full retry, `Load` for saved chats, `UnitTestChatConversation` with a scripted client and `TestChatConversationGemma4E2B`; `ReplyMarkdown` - the Markdown of replies read without UI, `UnitTestReplyMarkdown` - and the templated `ReplyView`, `CodeBlockView` and `ThinkingView`, which the demo's chat page uses with its own `CodeBlockViewTemplate`/`ThinkingViewTemplate` in `ChatTemplates.xaml`, merged in `App.xaml`; the controls find templates and styles by key in the app's resources, else `Themes/ChatStyles.xaml`, and bind their `PART_` elements in `OnApplyTemplate`; inside namespace `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.*`, `Chat` means the namespace `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat`, not `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Models.Chat`): `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat` (own package, `EMGU_LITERT_LM_CHAT_NUGET_ID`, props/README from `cmake/Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat.*.in`; `NetStandard/`, netstandard2.0 + net8.0, pure managed; depends on `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Extensions.AI`, `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Models`, `Microsoft.Extensions.AI.Abstractions`) holds the conversation logic without UI (`ChatConversation`, compaction, `ChatMessageItem`), and `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Maui` (own package, `EMGU_LITERT_LM_MAUI_CONTROL_NUGET_ID` - `EMGU_LITERT_LM_MAUI_NUGET_ID` is the LM runtime aggregator `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.runtime.maui` - props/README from `cmake/Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Maui.*.in`; `Maui/`, android/ios/maccatalyst, windows on Windows hosts, built by CMake on macOS/Windows only; depends on `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat` and `Microsoft.Maui.Controls`, no native runtime) the templated XAML control (`ChatView`, message templates, `Themes/ChatStyles.xaml`).
- Its shared `.projitems` lists `Themes/*.xaml` as `MauiXaml` items, since the MAUI SDK only finds XAML under the project's own folder.
- It also holds the voice classes (`VoiceRecorder`, `SpeechRecognizer`, `MicrophoneGate`; `ChatView.Voice.cs`).
- `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat.Sqlite` (own package, `EMGU_LITERT_LM_CHAT_SQLITE_NUGET_ID`, props/README from `cmake/Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat.Sqlite.*.in`; `NetStandard/`, netstandard2.0 + net8.0; depends on `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat` and `sqlite-net-pcl` 1.11.285, so `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat` itself has no SQLite dependency) holds the saved chats: `ChatHistoryStore` (`chats.db3` and `attachments/<chat id>/` in a folder; the demo's `Conversation`/`Message` tables, schema version 1 in a `SchemaVersion` table), `ChatHistory`, `ChatHistoryBinding` (`Attach`), tested by `UnitTestChatHistoryStore` (model-free).
- All three are in `Solution/CrossPlatform/` only.
- The `MauiChat` sample has a README.
