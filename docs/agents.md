# Agents and the reusable chat

`Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents` (the chat agent's tools), `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.CV` (visual agents for its camera monitors), `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.Mcp` (MCP tool servers as tools), `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.Documents` (documents read for questions, and the saved-document search) and `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Chat`, `.Chat.Sqlite` and `.Maui` (the reusable chat). Moved here from CLAUDE.md, which keeps the short version.

the model doesn't see them. One text because Gemma 4 E2B filled separate fields unreliably: "beach" as the place, invented cameras and kinds, asking for dates - 8/8 requests were passed on faithfully as one text. The parser handles today/yesterday/this or last week, weekend, month, year, seasons (northern hemisphere; "last" skips a season still under way), months with or without a year, "since/after/before 2023", years, "N weeks ago", "last N days"; kinds selfie/screenshot/panorama/favo(u)rite/portrait mode; places from the app's list or a capitalised name after in/from/at/to, plus country aliases (USA, UK, Holland...); cameras from the app's list or brand words; travel words dropped with a place; the rest minus filler words is the description), `PlaceLookup` (offline reverse geocoding from a gzip TSV of towns - the demo ships GeoNames' towns of 5,000+ people, CC BY 4.0, built by `Emgu.LiteRT.Example/Maui/tools/make_places.py` into `Resources/Raw/places.tsv.gz`, 1.1 MB, without PPLX/PPLH/PPLQ/PPLW; loads in ~0.1 s, lookups well under 1 ms through a 1° grid; the nearest town wins unless a more populous one is within 2 × its distance + 3 km, since big cities' districts are towns of their own - GeoNames' "Paris 16 Passy" was nearer the Eiffel Tower than central Paris; `Matches` ignores case, accents and punctuation), `Weather(http)` (Open-Meteo; `place`/`latitude`/`longitude` have defaults - without them `AIFunctionFactory` made the coordinates required), `Wikipedia(http)`, `TavilySearch(key, http)`/`TavilyReadPage(key, http)`, `CreateDocument(save, userRequest)` (`create_document`: title, Markdown content, format; `DocumentWriter.cs` writes Word .docx - Office Open XML by hand - PDF - a small A4 writer with the standard Helvetica/Courier fonts and WinAnsi text, falling back to Word for other scripts - or Markdown; the user's words choose the format first (`ChooseFormat`), and Markdown only when asked; the demo's `LiteRtLmChatPage.WrittenDocuments.cs` shows a card with Open/Share and saves the file with the reply as a `document` attachment, name in the media type; `UnitTestDocumentWriter`, TODO item 18), `ToolRouter`/`ToolGroup` (`ToolRouter.cs`: tool groups on demand - an app's groups come when a message's words need them (keywords at word starts - a keyword ending in `$` must end a word, so "a week$" misses "a weekend" - or `ToolGroup.AlsoMatches`) or the app calls `Activate`, and stay until `Reset` (a new conversation: LiteRT-LM re-reads the conversation when the tools change); every other group is a `use_{name}` stub plus one instruction line listing them, and calling a stub adds its tools and instructions to the same request through the options `currentOptions` returns - `FunctionInvokingChatClient.CurrentContext.Options`, which the function invocation re-reads each round - so the model calls them straight away; a group with `rerunAfterStub` (the demo's clock/calendar/maps) instead ends the request (`endRequest`) and `TakeRerun` tells the app to send the message again with the group - the demo sends it again into the same reply with `ChatConversation.RegenerateAsync` - since after the stub's turn Gemma 4 E2B asked for calendar details it had been given, while the camera planner did better continuing; the demo's groups are `ChatToolGroups.cs` + `LiteRtLmChatPage.Tools.cs`; `UnitTestToolRouting`, TODO item 37), `Goals(store)` (`AgentGoals.cs`: `add_goal`, `log_progress`, `get_goals`, `remove_goal` over an app's `IAgentGoalStore` - `InMemoryAgentGoalStore` - with the goal in the user's words: `GoalParser.Parse` reads target/unit/period ("three times a week", "20 pages a day", "4x per week"; weeks start on Monday; `GoalInstructionsAsync` lists each goal with this period's progress for the agent's instructions; `UnitTestAgentGoals`; `log_progress` takes the amount's unit too, since Gemma 4 E2B passed the 5 of "a run, 5 km" for a goal counted in times - another unit counts as one time; an hour without am/pm is the next one to come), since small models get exact dates wrong; unreadable or past times go back to the model; the app's `perform` gets a typed `DeviceActionRequest` (`Title`, `Details`, `ConfirmText`), should ask the user, and returns `DeviceActionDone`/`DeviceActionCancelled`/`DeviceActionFailed`; `DeviceActionInstructions(kinds)` belongs in the agent's instructions - without it Gemma 4 26B-A4B with thinking off said "I've set your alarm" without calling the tool; `UnitTestDeviceActions`), and `Describe(call)` for step lines; `AgentTools.DefaultHttpClient` (20 s, a User-Agent naming the package) unless an `HttpClient` is passed; request bodies via `Utf8JsonWriter`, `GetAsync`/`ReadAsStringAsync()` (netstandard2.0 has no `GetStringAsync(url, ct)`).

- Tests: `TestCalculator`, `TestUnitConverter`, `TestHtmlText`, `TestAgentMemoryTools` (no model, run by default), `TestAgentWebTools` (network: weather, Wikipedia, currency, the Ada Lovelace article read without its infobox or JSON) and `TestAgentTools` (Gemma 4 E2B calls `calculate` - 838,102.05 - `get_weather`, `convert_units` - 42.16 km - `convert_currency` and `read_web_page`; given a Wikipedia link it searched Wikipedia instead, so the page test uses python.org), the last two `TEST_MODELS`-gated.

Built-in skills in `Emgu.LiteRT.LM.Agents.Skills`: `CoreToolsSkill`, `MemorySkill(store)` (`LastGiven` for `MemoriesUsed`), `WebSkill` (`TavilyApiKey`, `SettingsLocation` in its wording), `GoalsSkill(store)` (one group, "goals", with the demo's keywords minus the exercise words, `AlsoMatches` on the user's goals loaded in `PrepareAsync`; `ExtraTools`/`ExtraInstructions`/`ExtraKeywords` for the demo's exercise counting to join it). `UnitTestChatSkills` (model-free).
